# Ultimate and referee sprite sheets

The four-cell WebP grids in this directory animate summons, clones, the referee and older effects. The six-cell sheets for Axe of Law, Third Eye and Lotus Ground Smash are in the cinematics directory. Anygma’s four poses use the supplied photo as their likeness reference.

The design uses public battle personas described in the cited profiles. All attacks, hit points, weapons and referee calls are fictional game mechanics.

[Anygma](https://www.uprisingrecords.com.ph/artists/anygma), FlipTop's founder and a battle host and commentator, appears as a fictional non-playable referee. His sprite raises a hand during introductions, signals Fight, calls the halt and points toward the round winner. The game uses its existing sound effects and text announcements.

Each ultimate costs 100 energy. Use **L** or the on-screen ultimate button. Training mode refills energy for practice. Blocking, armor, parries, spacing and missed hits can change the effective damage. The values above are for all hits connecting against a standing, unarmored opponent.

## Generation direction

The sheets use transparent cells and consistent floor anchors. The sprite sequences are listed below.

- Crown Smash: Crowned golden guardian, crown raised off the head, crown driven into the floor, guardian dissolving among gold fragments.
- Cerberus: Crouched summoning, raised heads, three fire jets and lingering embers.
- Third Eye: Eye-framing pose, energy gathering, release, closed orb, open orb and firing orb.
- Axe of Law: Closed law book, axe drawn from open book, chop, intact statue, diagonal crack and shattering stone.
- Triple Timer: Rotary gun, box-fed machine gun, heavy gun and twin compact guns. One generated clone per cell.
- Black Hole: Vortex seed, open black hole, collapsing spiral, last sparks.
- Shadow Meteor: The original jade arc and ground ripples sit beneath the dark transformation, shadow, and meteor effects.
- Triple Meaning: First blue seal, interlocking diamonds, triple detonation, cobalt glass fragments.
- Lotus Ground Smash: Lotus poses, muscular double raising both fists, ground slam and flying shards.
- Anygma: Hand raised, downward start signal, halt gesture, pointing to the winner. High bun, dark shirt, bead necklace and microphone based on the supplied photo.
- Casting sheet: Loonie prays with eyes closed, then opens them for the crown slam. Aklas raises his hand and sniffs a puff of green dust.
