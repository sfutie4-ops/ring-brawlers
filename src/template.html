ABOUT FLIPTAPWARS

FliptapWars is an unofficial, non-commercial fan-made game, made for fun as a tribute to Filipino battle rap culture.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by FlipTop Battle League, Uprising Records or any emcee shown. Characters are caricatures; every move, stat, power and storyline is fictional and not a statement about any real person.

The game is free to play. The optional “buy me a coffee” only supports development time — nothing in the game is sold.

Rights holder or featured artist and want something changed or removed? Use the suggestion box and it will be handled promptly.
