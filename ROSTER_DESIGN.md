# Caricature roster and skill design

Research basis: the nine official FlipTop emcee profiles linked below. The portrait from each profile was used as an identity reference for the generated caricature. The profile descriptions inform the game roles; the move names, combat powers, balance values, clothing color choices, and mechanics are fictional game-design interpretations. This is an unofficial fan-game roster, not an endorsed FlipTop product. The research used written profiles and portraits; it does not claim a frame-by-frame analysis of every battle.

## Shared balance rules

All fighters have 100 health, basic punch and kick, jump, guard, dash cooldown, a 20-energy upper-slot skill, a 30-energy wave, and a 100-energy super. Their differences are fictional balance choices and are not rankings of the artists' real skill.

The normal upper-slot animation is reused for Zaito's counter stance; the opening 0.34 seconds counter one incoming hit. Batas' uppercut armor lasts 0.28 seconds and reduces one hit to 60% damage without interrupting the move. Shehyee and Apekz marks last three seconds. Abra's slow lasts 0.9 seconds, Sinio's 0.6 seconds. Slow affects movement, not player input. Aklas' blink is 130 arena units forward with the shared 0.85-second dash cooldown.

## Caricature direction

Large expressive heads, compact adult bodies, inked silhouettes, and simplified cel shading. Recognizable reference features include Loonie's cap/neck tattoo and facial hair; Abra's tied-back hair; Shehyee's curls and intense brow; Batas' long beard and tattoos; Smugglaz' sunglasses and backward cap; Aklas' forehead and tied hair; Zaito's bald head, grin, and green jersey; Apekz' beanie and concentrated expression; and Sinio's cap, earrings, and expressive mouth. These are stylizations of the official reference photographs, not statements about personality or private conduct.
